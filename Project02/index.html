The Heritage of Ratnapura & Alluvial Mining

Sri Lanka (formerly Ceylon) is nicknamed Rathnadeepa—"The Island of Gems". For over 2,500 years, ethical traditional mining techniques have preserved gem deposits in alluvial riverbeds and ancient gravels.

Unlike heavy industrial open-pit mining, Sri Lankan gem extraction utilizes sustainable shaft mining and hand washing to protect local ecosystems while uncovering world-renowned untreated gems.

Evaluation Factors

Sri Lankan Gem Evaluation Guide

Understanding the key criteria when selecting natural Sri Lankan gemstones.

Color & Saturation

Ceylon gems are famous for bright, lively body colors that maintain high brilliance without turning dark or muted.

Clarity & Inclusions

Natural inclusions (like silk needles in sapphires) confirm genuine earth-mined origin and unheated status.

Custom Precision Cut

Lapidary artisans in Colombo and Ratnapura cut each stone to maximize internal refraction and carat retention.

NGJA Certification

Authentic gems are verified by National Gem & Jewellery Authority (NGJA) or GIA/GRS testing laboratories.

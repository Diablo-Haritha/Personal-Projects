The Heritage of Ratnapura & Alluvial Mining

Sri Lanka (formerly Ceylon) is nicknamed Rathnadeepa—"The Island of Gems". For over 2,500 years, ethical traditional mining techniques have preserved gem deposits in alluvial riverbeds and ancient gravels.

Unlike heavy industrial open-pit mining, Sri Lankan gem extraction utilizes sustainable shaft mining and hand washing to protect local ecosystems while uncovering world-renowned untreated gems.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on purchasing and importing gemstones directly from Sri Lanka.

How do I verify the authenticity of a gemstone?
Every genuine Sri Lankan gemstone can be certified by government-accredited laboratories like the National Gem & Jewellery Authority (NGJA) of Sri Lanka or independent GIA/GRS facilities.
How does buying via WhatsApp work?
When you click "Inquire", you will be connected directly to our Sri Lankan gem distributor via WhatsApp. You can request 360° videos under natural light, certification details, and pricing information instantly.
What is the difference between Heated and Unheated Sapphires?
Unheated sapphires are 100% natural, as taken from the earth without thermal treatment. Heated sapphires undergo traditional gentle heating to improve clarity and color consistency.
Can gemstones be legally exported from Sri Lanka?
Yes. All official exports are declared through Sri Lanka Customs and NGJA authorities, ensuring all legal and tax standards are strictly followed.
